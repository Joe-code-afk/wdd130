Rapids White Water Rafting

Our purpose is to give every guest a safe, thrilling, and unforgettable day on the river. Our motto is paddle hard, laugh louder, and respect the river.

History

Rapids White Water Rafting began with a small raft, a big river, and a love for adventure. From our first trip, we wanted to share the thrill of the rapids with everyone.

Today we guide families, friends, and first-time rafters down beautiful rapids while keeping safety first and fun always. Our guides are trained, experienced, and passionate about the river.
